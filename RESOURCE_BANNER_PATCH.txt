Because the uploaded ZIP does not include resources.html or the existing assets/style.css,
those files cannot be safely overwritten from this patch.

To activate the included thin-banner override on resources.html:

1. Change the body tag from:
   <body>
   to:
   <body class="resources-page">

2. Immediately after the existing assets/style.css link in <head>, add:
   <link rel="stylesheet" href="assets/banner-overrides.css">

The override makes content/resource images full-width but only 200px high on desktop
and 160px high on mobile, using object-fit: cover so they look like banners rather
than tall full pictures.
