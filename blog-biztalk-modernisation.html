Modernise integration without a big-bang migration

Protect business continuity by migrating capabilities in evidence-based waves—not by translating every BizTalk artefact one for one.

7 min readModernisation guide

A mature BizTalk environment often contains years of business rules, partner agreements, operational knowledge and hidden dependencies. The safest modernisation strategy first discovers what the platform actually does, then replaces capabilities according to business value and risk.

Build a factual integration inventory

Capture applications, schemas, maps, orchestrations, pipelines, adapters, certificates, trading partners, schedules, message volumes, service levels, incidents and owning teams. Runtime evidence is more reliable than solution lists alone because dormant and duplicate interfaces are common.

Classify before selecting technology

  • API and synchronous services: consider API Management, Functions or application services.
  • Workflow and orchestration: consider Logic Apps Standard or durable application workflows.
  • Messaging: consider Service Bus queues and topics with explicit retry and dead-letter handling.
  • Events: consider Event Grid or Event Hubs according to event semantics and scale.
  • B2B and files: assess partner protocols, EDI requirements, SFTP and managed file-transfer controls.

Design the target operating model

Modernisation is more than service substitution. Define environments, deployment pipelines, secret management, network connectivity, observability, support ownership, cost controls and architecture governance before moving critical flows.

Move in controlled waves

  1. Retire unused interfaces and consolidate duplicates.
  2. Start with low-risk, well-understood flows to prove the delivery pattern.
  3. Run parallel validation where data or operational risk is high.
  4. Migrate related capabilities together to avoid permanent bridging complexity.
  5. Decommission BizTalk artefacts only after evidence, support handover and rollback criteria are satisfied.
Avoid direct translation:

A BizTalk orchestration may become a Logic App, code-based workflow, event choreography—or no orchestration at all. Choose from the business and non-functional requirements.

Keep the controls that operations need

Preserve correlation, replay, message tracking, alerting, support dashboards and business-level reconciliation. Cloud-native services create stronger outcomes only when operational capability improves with the technology.

Measure the migration outcome

Track retired technical debt, reduced incident volume, improved recovery time, deployment frequency, interface lead time and platform cost. These measures reveal whether the program is creating a better integration capability rather than only moving hosting location.
